## Week 1 - History
- Epic of Gilgamesh - ~4000 years ago(2000 BC).
- Most European, Middle Eastern, and African alphabets evolved from Egyptian scripts.
- Johannes Gutenberg (Germany, ~1450s) did not invent printing, but perfected and popularized the movable type printing press in Europe.
- Typewriters ~150years ago(19th century).

## Week 2 - Bits and Bytes
- Morse Code: invented in the 1840s by Samuel Morse.
- Bit - binary digit.
- Byte - standard group of 8 bits.
- Ascii - American Standard Code for Information Interchange.

## Week 3 - Markup Languages
- Alphanumeric: letters and numbers.
- Alpha: letters.
- Escaping: tells the interpreter to read a special character literally.
  
## Week 4 - Keyboards & Editors
- Modifier Keys: have no function on their own. They only modify other keys.
- Touch typing - typing without looking at the keys.
- Word processors - rich text.
- Text editors - plain text.
- IDE - Integrated Development Environment.
  
## Week 5 - CLI
- CLI - Command Line Interface. Use to be the only way to interact with computers.
- SSH - Secure Shell
- grep command: used for searching plain-text data.
